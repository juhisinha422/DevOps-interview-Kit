Automate Jenkins Builds with Webhooks

Want your Jenkins jobs to run as soon as you push code? Use webhooks!

How It Works:

- Your Git provider sends a webhook to Jenkins when changes occur.

- Jenkins receives the payload and triggers the job automatically—no manual clicks needed.

Setup:

- Enable "GitHub hook trigger" in your job config.

- Add your Jenkins webhook URL to your repo’s webhook settings.

By leveraging webhooks, you can transform your Jenkins setup into a highly responsive, automated CI/CD engine, ensuring that your code is continuously integrated and tested with minimal human intervention.

Webhooks = faster feedback, cleaner CI/CD.
