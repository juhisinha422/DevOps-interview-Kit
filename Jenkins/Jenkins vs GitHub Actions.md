Jenkins vs GitHub Actions - Control vs Convenience.

This image explains CI/CD better than most tutorials.

Think of CI/CD like running a restaurant.

On the left is 𝐉𝐞𝐧𝐤𝐢𝐧𝐬.

On the right is 𝐆𝐢𝐭𝐇𝐮𝐛 𝐀𝐜𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧𝐬.

Both get the job done.
But the experience is very different.

𝐉𝐞𝐧𝐤𝐢𝐧𝐬 𝐢𝐬 𝐥𝐢𝐤𝐞 𝐫𝐮𝐧𝐧𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐲𝐨𝐮𝐫 𝐨𝐰𝐧 𝐫𝐞𝐬𝐭𝐚𝐮𝐫𝐚𝐧𝐭.

.You manage the kitchen, staff, equipment, and maintenance.
You decide how everything works.
It gives you deep control and flexibility - but also full responsibility.

This is why Jenkins shines in:

• Highly customized pipelines

• Complex enterprise workflows

• Teams that need full control over infrastructure

But yes, you also handle upgrades, plugins, scaling, and failures.

𝐆𝐢𝐭𝐇𝐮𝐛 𝐀𝐜𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧𝐬 𝐢𝐬 𝐥𝐢𝐤𝐞 𝐨𝐫𝐝𝐞𝐫𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐟𝐫𝐨𝐦 𝐚 𝐜𝐥𝐨𝐮𝐝 𝐤𝐢𝐭𝐜𝐡𝐞𝐧.

Everything is ready.

You just place the order and it gets delivered.

It works best when:

• Your code already lives on GitHub

• You want fast setup and minimal maintenance

• You prefer simplicity over deep customization

Less control, but much more convenience.

𝐓𝐡𝐞 𝐫𝐞𝐚𝐥 𝐢𝐧𝐭𝐞𝐫𝐯𝐢𝐞𝐰 𝐚𝐧𝐬𝐰𝐞𝐫 𝐢𝐬 𝐧𝐨𝐭 𝐰𝐡𝐢𝐜𝐡 𝐨𝐧𝐞 𝐢𝐬 𝐛𝐞𝐭𝐭𝐞𝐫.

The right answer is why you’d choose one over the other.

Senior engineers don’t pick tools emotionally.

They pick them based on context.

That’s the difference between knowing CI/CD
and actually designing CI/CD.
