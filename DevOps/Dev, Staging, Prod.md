Every mature engineering workflow relies on three key environments: Dev, Staging, and Prod.
They might sound similar, but they serve completely different purposes.

𝗗𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗹𝗼𝗽𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁 (𝗗𝗲𝘃)

This is where new features are built and tested locally by developers. It’s experimental, fast-moving, and mistakes are expected.

𝗦𝘁𝗮𝗴𝗶𝗻𝗴

This is a controlled copy of production used for final validation. Teams test integrations, performance, and configurations here to ensure everything behaves as expected before release.

𝗣𝗿𝗼𝗱𝘂𝗰𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 (𝗣𝗿𝗼𝗱)

This is the live environment where end users interact with the product. This is the “real world,” so stability, performance, and reliability are critical.

Clear separation between these environments helps teams catch issues early, maintain quality, and ship with confidence.
