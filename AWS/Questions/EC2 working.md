How Does Amazon EC2 Work?

Amazon EC2 (Elastic Compute Cloud) lets you rent virtual servers in the cloud.

 You can deploy apps, run workloads, and scale easily — all without managing physical hardware.

🔑 Key Highlights:

 ✨ Low-latency & high availability

 ✨ Secure with IAM, encryption & compliance

 ✨ Auto-scaling & elastic resources

 ✨ Fully integrated with AWS services

💡 Why EC2?

 ✅ Disaster recovery & multi-region failover

 ✅ Cost-effective pay-as-you-go pricing

 ✅ DevOps-ready with IaC & monitoring

 ✅ Hybrid cloud & flexible OS/instance types

👉 EC2 gives you the power of the cloud at your fingertips!
