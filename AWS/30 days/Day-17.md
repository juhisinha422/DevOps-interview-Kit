🚀 Day 17 – ECS vs EKS vs Lambda

 Title: ECS vs EKS vs Lambda – What Should You Choose? 🐳⚙️🛰️

key differences between AWS ECS, EKS, and Lambda — three powerful ways to run your applications on AWS. 

🔹 Amazon ECS (Elastic Container Service):

 ✅ Best for teams who want easy container orchestration without managing infrastructure

 ✅ Deeply integrated with AWS

 ✅ Simpler learning curve than Kubernetes

🔹 Amazon EKS (Elastic Kubernetes Service):

 ✅ Ideal for Kubernetes-savvy teams

 ✅ Full control over your cluster and deployments

 ✅ Best when you want to stick with open-source Kubernetes

🔹 AWS Lambda:

 ✅ Fully serverless — just write code and deploy

 ✅ Auto-scales instantly with zero infrastructure management

 ✅ Perfect for event-driven applications, APIs, or automation

💡 When to choose what?

Go with ECS for easy containerized apps

Choose EKS if you're already using or migrating Kubernetes

Use Lambda for small, modular, event-based tasks with minimal ops overhead

Each has its strength — your choice depends on use case, complexity, and team expertise!
