Day 26 – AWS Support Plans Explained
 Title: Choosing the Right AWS Support – Developer, Business, Enterprise On-Ramp & Enterprise

different AWS Support Plans and how they cater to various users — from solo developers to large enterprises with mission-critical workloads.

🧩 Support Tiers Overview:

🔹 Developer Support – For Individual Builders

 ✅ Ideal for testing and experimenting

 ✅ Email access to AWS Cloud Support during business hours

 ✅ Trusted Advisor (7 core checks), basic guidance

 ✅ Low cost – starting at $29/month

🔹 Business Support – For Production Workloads

 ✅ 24x7 phone, chat, and email support

 ✅ Access to AWS Trusted Advisor (all checks)

 ✅ Guidance on best practices, third-party software support

 ✅ Recommended for start ups and SMBs running live apps

🔹 Enterprise On-Ramp – For Rapidly Growing Businesses

 ✅ Faster response times with senior cloud support engineers

 ✅ Infrastructure event management (limited scope)

 ✅ Access to a Technical Account Manager (TAM-lite experience)

 ✅ Designed for businesses scaling toward enterprise level

🔹 Enterprise Support – For Mission-Critical Applications

 ✅ 24/7 access to senior cloud support engineers

 ✅ Full TAM (Technical Account Manager)

 ✅ Proactive guidance, reviews, and white-glove onboarding

 ✅ Access to AWS Infrastructure Event Management, Concierge Support

💡 Choosing the right support plan is crucial — it’s not just about fixing issues, but about building better, faster, and more confidently with AWS.
