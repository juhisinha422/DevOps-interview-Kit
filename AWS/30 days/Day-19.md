Day 19 – CloudFront (CDN) & S3 Website Hosting

 Title: Deliver Fast with AWS CloudFront + S3

Today I explored how to build and deliver lightning-fast websites using the powerful combo of Amazon S3 and CloudFront!

 🔹 Amazon S3 – A highly durable, cost-effective storage service that can also host static websites (HTML, CSS, JS) with ease.

 🔹 AWS CloudFront – A Content Delivery Network (CDN) that distributes your content across edge locations worldwide to minimize latency and maximize performance.

🌍 Why Use CloudFront with S3?

 ✅ Faster load times for users around the world

 ✅ Reduces origin load and improves scalability

 ✅ Supports SSL, custom domains, and access control

 ✅ Seamless integration with S3 for static content hosting

💡 With just a few steps, you can host your portfolio, blog, or product landing page on S3 and supercharge it with CloudFront for a blazing-fast experience.
