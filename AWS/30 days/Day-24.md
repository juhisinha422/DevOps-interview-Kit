📅 Day 24 – Mastering AWS Cost Management Tools 💰

🔖 Title: Day 24 – AWS Organizations, Billing & Cost Optimization Tools Explained!

💡 Day 24 of #30DaysOfCloud

Managing cloud resources is not just about building – it’s about spending smartly. Today I explored key AWS tools that help you manage accounts, track costs, and set budgets effectively:

🔹 AWS Organizations:

Used to manage multiple AWS accounts centrally, apply policies, and consolidate billing. Ideal for teams or businesses with separate environments (Dev, Prod, Test).

🔹 AWS Billing Dashboard:

Your go-to place to view current charges, payment history, and account-wide cost breakdown.

🔹 AWS Budgets:

Set custom budget alerts for usage, cost, or reserved instances. Get notified when usage or cost exceeds your threshold.

🔹 AWS Cost Explorer:

Powerful tool to analyze spending patterns over time, filter by service/account/region, and forecast future costs.

🔹 AWS Pricing Calculator:

Helps estimate the monthly cost of your architecture before launching resources. Great for planning and client proposals.

✅ These tools are essential not just for finance teams but also for cloud engineers who want to build efficiently and responsibly.
