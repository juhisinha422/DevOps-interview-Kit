In AWS, a Snapshot is a point-in-time backup of your EBS volume.
It plays a vital role in data protection, disaster recovery, and scaling.

🔹 Process Flow:
EC2 Instance → Snapshot → AMI → Launch New EC2

✅ Why use Snapshots?

	•	Backup critical data securely

	•	Restore volumes anytime

	•	Create AMIs for fasterdeployments

	•	Ensure business continuity

Cloud architecture becomes much easier when you understand how snapshots connect instances, backups, and recovery.
